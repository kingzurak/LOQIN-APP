      Safety Center

      How the Room keeps you safe, and how to protect yourself.

      Private groups run on trust: there are no automatic penalties, just tools. Anyone can report or block, and admins can remove people, rotate the invite code or close the group. Public groups put strangers together, so they add automatic protections.

      How to spot a scam

      • Anyone promising guaranteed returns, "x% weekly" or a "can't-lose" system.
      • Pressure to act fast, or to move the chat to WhatsApp or Telegram.
      • A "mentor", "account manager" or "recovery agent" who needs money first.
      • Requests for a wallet address, account number, OTP or password.
      • A brand-new member who is unusually friendly and quick to talk money.

      Real teammates never need your money to help you. If in doubt, tap and block them.
